Think better about real estate.

Technical knowledge is essential for successful investment, but it is rarely sufficient to produce consistently high-quality investment decisions.

Intrinsic Value Real Estate Advisers was established out of a desire to help investors, and investment managers, make progress through clear investment thinking and designing the organisation to produce consistently better investment decisions.

In an asset class prone to biases, accepted wisdoms and decisions justified by market practice, it is hard to distinguish signals from noise, to know what is useful and what is not, and, ultimately, to make progress. We aim to help by adopting and adapting the principles of value-investing to real estate, which we have been doing for over twenty years, as the basis for an intrinsic value investment framework, but also by blending broader evidence from research and behavioural insights into that framework.

We do this by providing consultancy to the real estate industry, and beyond — as a discrete project, or a longer-term strategic relationship. We can act as both sounding board and sparring partner.

Consulting

Sounding board. Sparring partner.

The main part of our business is consulting services. We aim to partner with groups, and individuals, and work with them to make progress on a broad range of issues covering:

Investment governance

(Fund) documentation

Investment approach and process

Investment risk and investment risk management

Decision making

Research and strategy

Our work aims to bring experience and diversity of thought to your group, helping to raise its potential by unpacking, (re-)building and developing your business in a clear and robust way, challenging the status quo where necessary and never accepting “market practice” as a rationale for a way of doing things. We aim to act as both sounding board and sparring partner.

Typical engagements

  1. 01

  3. 03

  4. 04

Training

“Learn from the mistakes of others. You can’t live long enough to make them all yourself.”

— Eleanor Roosevelt

As part of our services, we offer training, which is aimed to be both thought provoking and practical, for experienced and less-experienced real estate professionals and for those who rely upon real estate professionals’ views e.g. board directors. Training can be tailored to suit a business’ particular requirement.

Often unorthodox, our training aims to introduce concepts that may not be known and to debunk some that are well known, along with increasing self-awareness and the role of group dynamics in decision making. Attendees should leave with a set of tools to help them think more clearly about real estate investment, risk and decision making and to put in place processes to make better investment decisions. We don’t offer traditional training in real estate investment, nor do we run courses in financial modelling; our courses are an antidote to these.

Russell has run numerous training days across Europe and Asia for real estate senior managers, fund managers and transaction staff. Vicki has helped run a number of training days for industry professionals. Both Russell and Vicki bring their experience as both academics and practitioners to help think about simple approaches to complex issues.

We run in-house bespoke training days as well as courses.
